# Freelance Application Guidance

Freelance proposals and gig notes must follow the same factuality rules as CVs
and cover letters, with extra care for platform-specific claims.

## Allowed Content

- Verified profile facts.
- User-supplied portfolio references.
- Verified opportunity requirements.
- Clearly caveated setup or availability notes.

## Disallowed Content

- Invented platform ratings, job success scores, earnings, reviews, or client names.
- Unsupported claims about speed, availability, guarantees, or prior outcomes.
- Private messages, platform credentials, payment details, or secret tokens.

## Drafting Style

- Be concise and specific to the opportunity.
- Tie each claim to profile evidence or portfolio context.
- If evidence is missing, ask for confirmation or omit the claim.
