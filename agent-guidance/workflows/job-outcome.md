# Job Outcome Workflow

Canonical workflow for recording application results.

## Steps

1. Identify the application in `job_search_tracker.csv`.
2. Capture status, stage reached, feedback, offer details, rejection reason, and notes.
3. Archive submitted materials and posting text under `documents/applications/<company>_<role>/`.
4. Write or update `outcome.md` in the documented archive format.
5. Update tracker status without rewriting unrelated rows.

## Rules

- Record what happened; do not reinterpret the evaluation framework here.
- Never reconstruct expired postings from memory.
