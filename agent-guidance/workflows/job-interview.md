# Job Interview Workflow

Canonical workflow for preparing an interview.

## Steps

1. Identify the tracked application from `job_search_tracker.csv` or user input.
2. Load archived posting, submitted CV, submitted cover letter, and outcome notes when available.
3. Research current company/interviewer context and verify every factual claim.
4. Build a stage-specific prep pack under `documents/applications/<company>_<role>/`.
5. Map likely questions to STAR examples and honest gap-bridging answers.
6. Offer mock interview practice when useful.

## Rules

- Interview prep must match submitted application material.
- Do not invent experience to fill gaps.
